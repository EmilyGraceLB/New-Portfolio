The Technical Challenges & Solutions

Hurdle 1: Managing Unpredictable Data Drops in Real-Time Environments

The Problem: As a student researcher on a live project, navigating unexpected network drops or packet losses during real-time streaming was highly volatile.

The Solution: I assisted in setting up iterative troubleshooting test environments, monitoring how performance fluctuations altered the final stream output. This hands-on testing allowed me to document critical visual and audio safety boundaries to ensure system stability during live broadcasts.

Hurdle 2: Physics-Based Spatial Interaction

The Problem: Standard Raycast interaction models felt detached and broke immersion during spatial menu selection routines.

The Solution: Implemented a completely proximity-based physical collision system utilizing Unity's Input System events and physical trigger volumes to make buttons physically depress when touched.
